Sharpe is the average daily change in this $10,000 account, divided by the sample deviation of those daily changes, then multiplied by the square root of 365. A day with no close is a zero change. The count runs from each wallet's first close through its last, so the five numbers cover 185 to 256 days.

The fee is 1 bp of notional on the open and 1 bp on the close, 2 bp round trip. Notional is the margin closed times the leverage. Funding and the exchange fee schedule are not charged. Partial closes of one order share a single pile of margin. The resize uses the highest simultaneous margin in the book: $15,795 for 0xd49C, $32,850 for 0xDd49, $228,834 for 0xEDa7, $116,877 for 0xFFB7, and $231,303 for 0xdcE9.

The tape is Avantis closed-trade history. Veranta's board counts a few more closes, and 86 more for 0xDd49 (397 there, 311 here). Closes run from August 2025 through June 2026.
