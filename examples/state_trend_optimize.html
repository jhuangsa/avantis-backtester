StateTrend on BTC and ETH, 2026-06-01 to 2026-08-01

The optimizer searched these two months (207 backtests, base timeframe 1 minute) for the parameters with the highest Sharpe. This page shows only the searched period, so the "after" line is fitted to it.

What the strategy does

  1. Bias. Up when the last hourly close is above its average and the trend label is uptrend; down when it is below and the label is downtrend.
  2. Entry. With the bias, when the market label is a trend that way or a breakout, the 1-minute close breaks the high (low) of the prior breakout minutes, and volatility is not extreme or a shock. The order fills at the next minute's open.
  3. Stop and take profit. The stop is a number of hourly ATRs from the entry (ATR: the average size of one bar's range); the take profit is twice that. No entry when the stop would be closer than the min stop, so it skips calm hours.
  4. Exit. When the bias no longer holds or the market label turns to the opposite trend.
  5. Flip. When on, every trade takes the other side: an upward breakout opens a short.
