avantis-backtester

Test your day-trading setup on real minute bars before you trade it

Write your entry, stop, target and sizing rules once. The backtester replays real Avantis prices minute by minute, trades a simulated account with your rules, and shows you the P&L, the trades and the drawdown.

What it can do

Everything a day trader sets on a trade

Entries

Long, short, or both

Enter on any rule you can describe: a breakout, an indicator cross, a time of day, a market regime. Orders fill at the next bar's open.

Stops

Stop loss

As a % of price or in ATRs. Checked against every minute's high and low. A gap through your stop fills at the gap price.

Stops

Trailing stop

Follows price by a set gap (say 2 ATR) and never moves back. Fills at the stop, inside the bar.

Targets

Take profit at NR

Set the target as a multiple of your risk: 1R, 2R, 3R, any N.

Targets

Scale out

Sell part of the position at the target (say half at 2R) and let the rest run on the trailing stop.

Sizing

Risk % per trade

Size from risk and stop distance: risk 1% of the account and the size is worked out from where your stop sits.

Leverage

Leverage and liquidation

Pick leverage per trade, and have the risk grow with it: 5x makes and loses 5 times as much. Positions liquidate at Avantis's real level (85% of margin lost).

Account

Max-loss kill switch

If the account drops to a set % below its start, everything closes and trading stops. Open P&L counts.

Costs

Fees on every fill

Opening and closing fees come out of every trade, so scalps that only win the spread show up as losers.

Timeframes

Multi-timeframe rules

Read the 4H or daily trend while entering on 1-minute or 15-minute bars, in one rule.

Markets

Many markets, one account

Trade BTC, ETH and more side by side from one balance, one position per market.

Tuning

Optimizer

Give it the settings to try. It tests the combinations and hands back the best one by Sharpe.

Example setup

StateTrend: a breakout setup on BTC and ETH

A ready-made setup that shows the pieces working together. Tested on June and July 2026, on 1-minute bars, with a $10,000 account.

1

Bias

Long bias when the hourly close is above its 20-hour average and Avantis labels the market an uptrend. Short bias is the reverse.

2

Trigger

The 1-minute close breaks the high (or low) of the last 60 minutes, in a trend or breakout regime, and volatility isn't extreme.

3

Stop and target

Stop at 2 hourly ATRs; target at 2R. Skip the trade if the stop would be closer than 2%.

4

Exit

Out as soon as the bias flips or the regime turns against the trade. The tuned version fades the breakout instead of following it.

Leverage and size

What leverage actually changes

The same tuned StateTrend at 1x, 5x, 10x, 20x, 30x and 50x. Each trade at 5x makes, and loses, 5 times as much as at 1x.

LeverageTradesReturnSharpeMax drawdown

Account value at each leverage, June to July

Returns grow a bit faster than the leverage, because each win makes the next trade bigger. Drawdowns grow with them: at 20x the account fell 25% from a peak. Sharpe stays about 10 up to 20x, because gains and swings scale together.

Past 20x, liquidation gets in the way. The higher the leverage, the smaller the move that liquidates you: 4.25% at 20x, 2.8% at 30x, 1.7% at 50x. The backtester refuses any trade whose stop is at or near its liquidation price, just as Avantis does. StateTrend's stops are at least 2% away, so at 30x only 65 of its 115 trades fit, and the return drops to +92%. At 50x none fit, and it doesn't trade at all.

Under the hood

A Python engine for quick single-setup tests and a fast C++ engine for multi-market, multi-timeframe accounts and the optimizer. The engine never sees future bars: a rule decides on a closed bar and fills at the next open. Prices and market labels come from Avantis.
