# A bar with both levels exits at the stop

The high and the low of a bar can hit a take profit or a stop loss. The fill is the level, or the open if the bar opens already through it. When both levels sit inside the same bar, the stop loss is the exit, because the series does not say which price came first and the target may never have been traded. Treating the levels as close-only tests was the alternative, and that is a rule exit under another name. Dropping the trade was the other alternative, and it throws out the wide bars where the doubt is largest.
