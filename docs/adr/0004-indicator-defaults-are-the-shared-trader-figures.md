# Indicator defaults are the shared trader figures

The lines are the 37 in `docs/research/indicators.md`. Where traders share a default, that figure is pinned, including MACD 12/26/9, RSI 14, Bollinger 20 and 2, stochastic 14/3/3, Supertrend 10 and 3, and average true range 14. Where they do not share one, the hypothesis must name the window. Library defaults are not used. They disagree with each other, and one of them gives Bollinger bands a length of 5, which would move a grid without anyone choosing it.
