# A hypothesis may name its position from closed bars

Superseded in part by ADR 0007. The entry form may omit the take profit, and an entry may be an above or a below.

The day-up, hour-down rule is not a cross and names no target. A second object beside the hypothesis would make one backtest into two nouns. A hypothesis still names entries, a target, and a stop, or it names long, short, or flat from bars that have already closed. That position fills at the next bar's open. The entry form still requires a target and a stop.
