# An entry may be a state, and the target may be omitted

Breakout-30 gets in when the close is already beyond the prior channel, and it leaves by a stop or a fixed number of bars. It names no take profit. Requiring a cross and a target made that rule inexpressible. An above or a below may open a trade while the hypothesis is flat. The stop stays required, so a trade still has a stated loss. A cross entry and a named take profit score as they did before. This supersedes the target half of ADR 0002 and the entry-form sentence of ADR 0006.
